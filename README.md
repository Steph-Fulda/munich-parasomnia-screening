# Munich Parasomnia Screening (MUPS)

This repository packages the Munich Parasomnia Screening (MUPS), a 21-item self-report questionnaire for adult parasomnias and other nocturnal behaviors. It restores author-supplied companion materials that were historically available with the publication but were not retained as supplements after the journal moved to Springer.

## Language and validation status

The German questionnaire is the validated instrument represented here. The English questionnaire and English instructions are translations prepared for illustration and accessibility; they have **not** been independently validated and must not be presented as validated English versions.

The German explanatory document is provided alongside the German questionnaire to preserve the original materials and instructions.

## Recommended use

Use the German questionnaire when relying on the published validation evidence. The English materials may be used to understand the layout and item content, but they should not be used as evidence of English-language measurement validity. This repository is a document archive and does not provide diagnosis, scoring advice, or clinical recommendations.

## Publication

Please cite the original validation article when using the MUPS:

> Fulda, S., Hornyak, M., Müller, K., Cerny, L., Beitinger, P. A., & Wetter, T. C. (2008). Development and validation of the Munich Parasomnia Screening (MUPS): A questionnaire for parasomnias and nocturnal behaviors. *Somnologie, 12*, 56–65. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11818-008-0336-x
