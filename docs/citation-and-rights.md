# Citation and rights review

## Recommended citation

Fulda, S., Hornyak, M., Müller, K., Cerny, L., Beitinger, P. A., & Wetter, T. C. (2008). Development and validation of the Munich Parasomnia Screening (MUPS): A questionnaire for parasomnias and nocturnal behaviors. *Somnologie, 12*, 56–65. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11818-008-0336-x

The article describes the development and validation of the MUPS in adult participants. The validation evidence applies to the German instrument; it should not be generalized to the illustrative English translation included here.

## Before publication

Before a public release, record the following in the repository:

1. permission to redistribute the German questionnaire and German instructions;
2. permission and attribution requirements for the English translation;
3. whether the original PDF files must remain unchanged;
4. whether a repository-level license is appropriate; and
5. the preferred citation and contributor credit for the translation.
